## 1. 🌟 100% Zero-Cutoff Fitting Modes

1. **Ambient Gaussian Blur Fill**: Uncropped center video with $30\text{px}$ color-matched ambient blur padding.
2. **2.5D Depth Parallax Rig**: Separates foreground character ($Z = -250\text{px}$) from background ($Z = +150\text{px}$) with counter-directional drift.
3. **Ken Burns Panoramic Scan**: Glides across $100\%$ width of wide photos and footage over clip duration.
4. **Stacked Duplex**: Vertical split stacking Left Subject on Top and Right Subject on Bottom.
5. **Glassmorphic Elevated Card**: Floating rounded card with specular rim light and realistic depth drop shadows.

## 3. 👁️ Gaze-Direction Lead-Room Solver

Calculates natural cinematic breathing space:
- When subject looks **Left**: Virtual camera pans right by $22\%$ of frame width to allocate left lead room.
- When subject looks **Right**: Virtual camera pans left by $22\%$ of frame width.
- Always preserves a minimum of $12\%$ top headroom.
